## Circuit board in Tucson

A circuit board, called a control board or PCB, is a printed circuit board that controls the electrical functions of an air conditioner, directing power to the compressor, fan motors, relays and sensors so every component runs in the right sequence at the right time. Circuit Board is a type of Printed Circuit Board adapted for HVAC use, carrying traces, capacitors, resistors, relays and a microcontroller on a single laminated panel rated to handle the voltage swings and heat cycles inside a residential or light-commercial AC unit. ### Parts that make up an AC unit circuit board

A circuit board is built from several discrete components, each with a defined job inside the control panel. The microcontroller reads thermostat signals and sends switching commands. Relays open and close the high-voltage paths to the compressor and fan motors, and capacitors smooth the power supply so voltage spikes do not corrupt the microcontroller's logic. Resistors set reference voltages across the board, and the copper traces carry current between every part.

- **Microcontroller:** Microcontroller interprets thermostat input and sequences the start and stop commands for every load on the system.

- **Relays:** Relays are electromechanical switches on the board that open and close the circuits feeding the compressor and blower motor.

- **Capacitors:** Capacitors filter voltage ripple and protect the microcontroller from transient spikes during compressor startup.

- **Resistors:** Resistors divide and limit voltage so sensor signals arrive at the microcontroller within its readable range.

- **Traces:** Traces are the copper pathways etched into the board that carry current between components, rated from 0.5 A to 5 A depending on the circuit.

### Measurable attributes of a control board

A control board carries several rated specifications that determine which unit it fits and how it performs under Arizona's heat. Voltage rating runs from 12 V to 24 V DC on the low-voltage control side, while relay outputs switch line-voltage loads. Temperature range matters here. The operating range spans from -40°C to 85°C in Tucson where attic-mounted air handlers regularly push ambient temperatures toward the upper limit of the board's operating range, shortening the service life of capacitors and relay contacts. Trace current capacity ranges from 0.5 A to 5 A per circuit, and the number of relays and terminals varies by system size, with multi-stage and variable-speed units carrying more switching points than single-stage models. - **Voltage rating:** Voltage rating is 12 V to 24 V DC on the control side, matching the low-voltage wiring that runs between the thermostat and the air handler.

- **Operating temperature range:** Operating temperature range is -40°C to 85°C, the window within which the board's components stay within spec.

- **Trace current capacity:** Trace current capacity is 0.5 A to 5 A, set by the width and thickness of each copper trace on the board.

- **Temperature resistance:** Temperature resistance describes the board's ability to hold its rated performance at the upper end of its operating temperature range without delaminating or drifting in resistance.

- **Number of relays and terminals:** Number of relays and terminals scales with system complexity. A two-stage system needs more relay positions than a single-speed unit.

### How the circuit board relates to the broader cooling system

A circuit board controls the operation of the air conditioner as a whole, sitting between the thermostat signal and every powered load in the system. When the thermostat calls for cooling, the PCB closes the relay to the compressor contactor, starts the indoor blower and monitors safety inputs from the high-pressure switch and the limit sensor simultaneously. A [central air conditioning system](https://air-zona-heating-cooling.mintlify.app/central-air-conditioning-system) depends on the control board to coordinate those actions in the correct order. Without it, the compressor and fan run independently or not at all. Repair or replacement requirements can vary and should be confirmed with the local building authority. ### Common signs of circuit board failure

Circuit board failure shows up as a pattern of symptoms. No single mechanical fault explains the full picture on its own. The system may short-cycle, starting and stopping every few minutes, because the microcontroller is reading a corrupted sensor signal. Burned or discolored spots on the board itself are a direct sign of a failed relay or a trace that carried more current than its rating. A faulty circuit board can cause the blower to run continuously while the compressor stays off, or prevent the unit from responding to thermostat commands at all. [AC Short Cycling](https://air-zona-heating-cooling.mintlify.app/ac-short-cycling) frequently traces back to board-level faults.

- **Short cycling:** Short cycling occurs when the board's logic loop trips a safety shutdown on a false reading, restarting the call for cooling within minutes.

- **No response to thermostat:** No response to thermostat signals means the board is not receiving or not processing the 24 V call from the stat.

- **Burned relay or trace:** A burned relay or trace is visible on inspection and indicates a current overload that exceeded the board's 5 A trace rating.

- **Blower runs, compressor does not:** Blower runs while the compressor stays off when the relay controlling the compressor contactor has failed open.

- **Intermittent cooling:** Intermittent cooling points to a board that functions when cool but drops out as the operating temperature climbs toward the upper end of its 85°C range.

### Where a faulty circuit board leads. Repair or replacement

A faulty circuit board that has a single burned relay can sometimes be repaired by replacing that relay alone, but a board with multiple failed traces or a damaged microcontroller is replaced as a unit. Replacement boards must match the original voltage rating, relay count and terminal layout exactly, or the new board will not communicate correctly with the thermostat or the compressor contactor. When a board fails in a system that is already aging, the cost of the board itself often makes [AC unit replacement](https://air-zona-heating-cooling.mintlify.app/ac-unit-replacement) the more practical path, particularly if the refrigerant circuit or compressor shows wear. A licensed [air conditioning contractor](https://air-zona-heating-cooling.mintlify.app/air-conditioning-contractor) reads the fault codes stored in the microcontroller before condemning a board, because a wiring fault or a failed sensor can produce the same symptoms as a dead PCB. In some cases [AC replacement](https://air-zona-heating-cooling.mintlify.app/ac-replacement) is the right call when the board failure is one of several compounding issues in an older system.
