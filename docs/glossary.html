Glossary — AirZona Heating & Cooling LLC

Promotional and educational coverage of AirZona Heating & Cooling LLC. Not operated by AirZona Heating & Cooling LLC.

  • AC System Diagnostics: AC System Diagnostics, called Air Conditioning Diagnostics, is a systematic evaluation of an air conditioning unit that identifies faults, performance issues and the root causes behind them.
  • AC Refrigerant Recharge: AC Refrigerant Recharge, called an Air Conditioner Refrigerant Recharge, is the process of adding refrigerant to an air conditioning system to restore the pressure and charge level the system needs to cool a space, covering refrigerant measurement, pressure testing, leak assessment and the actual addition of refrigerant gas to bring the system back to its rated operating charge.
  • AC Electrical System Diagnosis: AC Electrical System Diagnosis is systematic testing of the electrical circuits and components inside an air conditioning unit, covering circuit breakers, wiring, capacitors, the wiring harness and individual control circuits to locate the fault causing a cooling failure or erratic operation.
  • R-410A Refrigerant Service: R-410A Refrigerant Service, called Puron Service, is the specialized procedure for recovering, evacuating, and recharging R-410A refrigerant in a residential or light commercial air conditioning system.
  • R-32 Refrigerant Service: R-32 Refrigerant Service, called R32 AC recharge, is the maintenance and repair procedure applied to air conditioning systems that use R-32 as their working refrigerant, covering refrigerant recovery, pressure testing, leak detection, vacuum pull-down and refrigerant recharge to restore a system to its rated cooling capacity.
  • Evaporator Coil Cleaning: Evaporator coil cleaning is the removal of dirt, dust, mold, and biological buildup from the indoor coil of an air conditioning system.
  • Thermal Expansion Valve: A thermal expansion valve, called a thermostatic expansion valve or TXV, is a refrigeration valve that meters refrigerant flow into the evaporator coil by reading superheat at the evaporator outlet, adjusting an internal orifice in real time to match the load on the system, and preventing liquid refrigerant from reaching the compressor.
  • TXV Valve: A TXV Valve, short for Thermostatic Expansion Valve, is a refrigerant metering device.
  • AC Pressure Switch: An AC pressure switch, called a pressure sensor switch, is an electrical switch that opens or closes a circuit based on refrigerant pressure levels inside an air conditioning system.
  • Compressor Failure Repair: Compressor failure repair, called Compressor Unit Repair, is the diagnosis and restoration of a non-functional air conditioner compressor, covering refrigerant recovery, component inspection, parts replacement and system recharging to return the unit to full cooling operation.
  • Condenser Fan Motor Replacement: Condenser fan motor replacement is the removal of a failed or failing fan motor from the outdoor condenser unit and the installation of a compatible new motor, including reconnecting wiring, reattaching the fan blade, verifying rotation direction and speed, and confirming that the refrigerant circuit returns to normal operating pressure once the motor runs.
  • Capacitor Replacement: Capacitor replacement, called a capacitor swap, is the removal of a worn or failed AC capacitor and the installation of a new one to restore the start and run function of an air conditioning system.
  • Contactor Replacement: Contactor replacement, called an AC contactor swap, is the removal of a failed electrical switching component inside an air conditioner and the installation of a compatible new unit in its place.
  • Circuit Board: A circuit board, called a control board or PCB, is a printed circuit board that controls the electrical functions of an air conditioner, directing power to the compressor, fan motors, relays and sensors so every component runs in the right sequence at the right time.
  • Split System Air Conditioner: A split system air conditioner, called a split AC, is a type of air conditioning system that separates the condensing unit outside from one or more evaporator heads mounted inside, connected by refrigerant lines, electrical wiring and a condensate drain.
  • Ductless Mini Split System Repair: Ductless Mini Split System Repair, called Ductless Air Conditioner Repair, is the diagnosis and hands-on correction of faults in ductless heating and cooling systems, covering refrigerant leaks, compressor failures, sensor malfunctions, drainage blockages, electrical faults and airflow restrictions across both the indoor air-handler and the outdoor condenser unit.
  • Packaged HVAC System Cooling Repair: Packaged HVAC system cooling repair, called Packaged Unit AC Repair, is the diagnosis and restoration of cooling function in self-contained rooftop or ground-mounted units.
  • Heat Pump Cooling Repair: Heat pump cooling repair, called Heat Pump AC Repair, is the diagnosis and correction of faults that prevent a heat pump from delivering cool air, covering refrigerant leaks, compressor failures, reversing valve malfunctions, defrost control errors, capacitor failures, fan motor problems and thermostat miscommunication in both residential and light commercial systems.
  • Cooling Thermostat Calibration: Cooling thermostat calibration, called Temperature Sensor Adjustment, is the adjustment of a cooling thermostat to produce accurate temperature readings and reliable on/off control of an air conditioning system.
  • AC Condensate Drain Line: An AC condensate drain line, called a condensate line, is an HVAC system component that channels water produced during air conditioning operation away from the indoor unit.
  • Electrical Connections: Electrical connections, called electrical terminals or electrical joints, are the points where wires, conductors, and terminal hardware join inside an air conditioning system.
  • Air Filter: An air filter, called a furnace filter or particulate filter, is a porous medium installed inside an HVAC system that captures airborne particles, including dust, pollen, pet dander, mold spores and fine debris, before they circulate through the ductwork and into living spaces.
  • Drainage System: A drainage system, called a Water Drainage System, is the network of components in an air conditioning unit that collects, channels and removes the condensate water produced when warm indoor air passes over a cold evaporator coil, including the drain pan, condensate line, drain pipe, float switch and condensate pump working together to keep moisture from pooling inside the unit or spilling onto floors and ceilings.
  • AC Not Cooling: AC Not Cooling, called Cooling Deficiency, is a measurable failure state in which a central air conditioning system runs but does not reduce indoor air temperature to the thermostat setpoint, covering conditions where the temperature differential across the evaporator coil drops to near 0°F, airflow at supply registers falls to near zero CFM, refrigerant pressure deviates outside the expected operating range, the compressor cycles off prematurely or stops entirely, and the air leaving the vents stays at or above the indoor ambient temperature rather than 15°F to 20°F below it.
  • No Cool Air: No cool air, called no cooling, is a condition where an air conditioning system runs but fails to lower the indoor air temperature.
  • Cooling Loss Diagnosis: Cooling loss diagnosis, called Insufficient Cooling Analysis, is the systematic process of identifying why an air conditioning system is not producing the expected cooling output, covering temperature differential measurement, static pressure testing, airflow rate evaluation, refrigerant pressure analysis, visual inspection of components and leak detection across the full system.
  • AC Short Cycling: AC short cycling, called rapid cycling AC, is an air conditioning fault where the compressor turns on and off too frequently.
  • High Head Pressure: High head pressure, called high discharge pressure, is an air conditioning condition where refrigerant pressure on the high-pressure side of the system rises above the normal operating range, forcing the compressor to work harder, raising discharge temperatures, reducing cooling capacity and accelerating wear on every component downstream of the compressor.
  • Low Suction Pressure: Low suction pressure, called low PSI, is a type of AC system problem where the refrigerant pressure in the suction line falls below the nominal operating range for the system, causing reduced cooling capacity, compressor strain and potential equipment damage across residential and light commercial air conditioning systems.
  • Frozen Evaporator Coil: A frozen evaporator coil, called an AC coil freeze-up, is an air conditioning condition in which ice builds up on the coil's tubing and fin plates.
  • Clogged Condensate Drain Line: A clogged condensate drain line, called a blocked condensate line, is a type of air conditioning issue in which algae slime, mineral deposits, biofilm, debris and accumulated water block the pipe that carries moisture away from the indoor air handler, stopping the system from removing humidity, triggering water damage to ceilings and walls, shutting down the unit on a float switch, and in some cases allowing mold to grow inside the drain pan and ductwork.
  • Coolant Leak: A coolant leak, called an AC leak or refrigerant leak, is the escape of refrigerant fluid from a closed air conditioning system.
  • Licensed HVAC Contractor: A licensed HVAC contractor, called an HVAC company, is a state-authorized individual or business legally permitted to install, maintain, and repair heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems in residential and commercial buildings.
  • Manufacturer Warranty: A manufacturer warranty, called an OEM Warranty or Factory Warranty, is a guarantee against defects in materials and workmanship issued by the equipment maker for a specified period.
  • HVAC Repair Warranty: A warranty on HVAC repair work typically covers the quality of the repair and the parts used in it for a defined period after the job is complete.
  • System Calibration: System calibration, called system tuning, is the adjustment of an air conditioning or HVAC system to manufacturer-specified operating parameters.
  • SEER Rating Impact on Repair: SEER Rating Impact on Repair, called Seasonal Efficiency Impact, describes how a central air conditioning unit's Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio score shapes the difficulty, cost, and parts availability of every repair that unit will ever need.
  • NATE Certified AC Technician: A NATE Certified AC Technician, called a NATE Certified HVAC Technician, is a field technician who has passed a proctored, third-party exam administered by North American Technician Excellence.
  • Air Conditioning Circuit Breaker: An air conditioning circuit breaker, called an AC breaker, is an electrical safety device that automatically interrupts power to an AC unit when it detects a fault condition such as an overload, a short circuit, a ground fault or a sustained current draw that exceeds the breaker's rated amperage.
