### Scope and component parts of ductless mini split system repair

Ductless Mini Split System Repair is a type of Air Conditioning Repair Service. It covers every physical part of a split-ductless system: the indoor evaporator air-handler, the outdoor condenser, the refrigerant lines connecting them, the condensate drain, the control board, and the remote or thermostat. Each component can fail independently. A complete repair call addresses whichever part has failed rather than the system as a whole. Requirements can vary and should be confirmed with the EPA. Mini Split Repair differs from ducted central AC repair in one structural way: there is no ductwork to inspect, seal or balance. ### Common problems that require ductless AC service

Five recurring faults trigger most ductless mini split system repair calls. The signs and their causes are listed below.

- **No cooling output:** No cooling output usually points to a low refrigerant charge from a leak in the line set or at a flare fitting, or to a failed compressor that no longer circulates refrigerant.

- **Water dripping from the air-handler:** Water dripping from the air-handler means the condensate drain line is blocked, causing the drain pan to overflow onto the wall or floor below the unit.

- **Unit short-cycling:** Short-cycling, where the system starts and stops every few minutes, is commonly caused by a failing capacitor, a dirty coil or a refrigerant charge that is too high or too low. A related service is [capacitor replacement](https://air-zona-heating-cooling.mintlify.app/capacitor-replacement), which resolves the electrical side of this fault.

- **Ice on the indoor coil:** Ice on the indoor coil forms when airflow is blocked by a clogged filter or when refrigerant is undercharged, both of which drop coil temperature below the freezing point of condensate.

- **Error codes on the display:** Error codes signal a fault the control board has detected, ranging from a communication error between the indoor and outdoor units to a thermistor reading outside its expected range.
