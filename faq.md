## What is a thermal expansion valve?

A thermal expansion valve, called a thermostatic expansion valve or TXV, is a refrigeration valve that meters refrigerant flow into the evaporator coil by reading superheat at the evaporator outlet, adjusting an internal orifice in real time to match the load on the system, and preventing liquid refrigerant from reaching the compressor. ## What is a TXV valve?

A TXV Valve, short for Thermostatic Expansion Valve, is a refrigerant metering device. It regulates how much liquid refrigerant enters the evaporator coil, controls evaporator superheat, and responds to suction-line temperature and pressure conditions. The valve adjusts flow in real time to match the cooling load through a mechanical assembly of a diaphragm, sensing bulb, needle, seat, and orifice. It sits at the inlet of the evaporator. ## What is an AC pressure switch?

An AC pressure switch, called a pressure sensor switch, is an electrical switch that opens or closes a circuit based on refrigerant pressure levels inside an air conditioning system. It protects the compressor, condenser, and refrigerant lines from damage caused by pressure that runs too high or drops too low. The switch monitors the refrigerant circuit continuously, cutting power to the compressor the moment pressure moves outside a safe operating band. Most residential systems carry at least two. A high-pressure switch and a low-pressure switch are each set to a different activation threshold. Either switch trips and the system shuts down rather than operate in a condition that destroys the compressor. Parts of an AC pressure switch include a housing, pressure port, diaphragm, pressure sensor, electrical contacts and wiring terminals.
