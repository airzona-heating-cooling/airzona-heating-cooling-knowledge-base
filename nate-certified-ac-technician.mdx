A NATE Certified AC Technician covers the full range of residential and light-commercial cooling work: diagnosing refrigerant leaks, testing electrical components, measuring airflow through supply and return ductwork, calibrating thermostats, evaluating compressor performance and verifying that a central air conditioning system is operating within manufacturer specifications.
